ABOUT KGS

KGS Construction & Transport is a Grade C7 registered construction company with over 15 years of proven experience building Sri Lanka's infrastructure. Founded in 2011, we have grown from a local contractor into a trusted name across the island for road concreting, building construction, large-scale land development, and heavy transport.
